Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What information we collect

When you register a student through this website, we collect the student's name, date of birth, gender, and academic details, along with the parent/guardian's name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and home address. We collect only what is needed to process the registration and manage the student's school record.

When you submit a review, we collect your name, your rating and comment, and optionally your email address (which is never displayed publicly).

When you make an online payment, payment processing is handled by our payment provider, Campay — we never see or store your Mobile Money PIN or full account credentials. We store only the amount, date, and a transaction reference for our own financial records.

How we use this information

  • To process student registrations and maintain accurate school records.
  • To generate invoices, process payments, and issue receipts.
  • To contact parents/guardians about registration status, fees, or school matters.
  • To moderate and, where approved, publish reviews on this website.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Staff and student portal accounts

Manager, Accountant, Teacher, Employee, and Student portal logins are created internally by school staff — there is no public account signup on this website. Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing (bcrypt) and are never visible to anyone, including school staff, in readable form.

Cookies

This site uses a strictly necessary session cookie to keep staff/student portal logins working after signing in. With your consent (via the banner shown on your first visit), we also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site — see our Cookie Policy for details.

Data retention

Student, financial, and employment records are retained for as long as needed for the school's administrative and legal record-keeping purposes. You may contact us to request correction of inaccurate information about you.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be directed to the school at the contact details on our Contact page.
