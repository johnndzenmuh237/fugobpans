Our Objectives

Full Gospel Bilingual Nursery and Primary School is committed to giving every child a strong foundation in a Fun, secure and child-centred environment. We are dedicated to a fully bilingual system, providing English and French education to prepare children who are well-equipped and qualified to meet the demands of primary and nursery education in Cameroon.

Location

FUGOBPANS is located at Mundani, after carrefour Graceland (opposite Full Gospel Church). We offer the complete cycle and a bilingual system.
