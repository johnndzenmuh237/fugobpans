Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Strictly necessary cookies

We set one cookie, named session, only when a Manager, Accountant, Teacher, Employee, or Student signs into their portal. It keeps you logged in and cannot be disabled while still using the portal — without it, you would need to re-enter your password on every page. This cookie is not set for ordinary visitors browsing the public website.

Optional analytics cookies

With your consent (via the banner on your first visit), we use Google Analytics to understand which pages are visited and how, so we can improve the website. Google Analytics cookies do not identify you personally — we do not send names, emails, or student information to Analytics. You can decline this at any time by clearing the banner choice stored in your browser and choosing "Decline" again, or by using your browser's cookie/site-data settings to remove stored preferences for this site.

Your choice

You can accept or decline analytics cookies from the banner shown when you first visit. Declining does not affect your ability to browse the site, register a student, or make a payment.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the school via our Contact page.
